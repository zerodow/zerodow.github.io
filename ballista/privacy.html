Privacy Policy

Ballista: Brick Breaker (Android, package com.kelvin.ballista)
Effective date: 11 November 2026 (2026-11-11)

In short
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game uses Google services for rewarded ads (Google AdMob), usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). These receive device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, how you use the app, and diagnostics, as described below.
  • The game is not directed at children under 16.

1. Who we are

Ballista: Brick Breaker ("the game") is made by Kelvin, an independent developer ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email minhducc96@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps a save file on your device with your progress: cleared islands, best scores, settings, energy, tools, tutorial steps and a run in progress. It is not sent to us, and the game turns off Android's app-data backup, so it is not copied to your Google account. We do not run any server. The save file is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.

3. Data collected by Google services in the game

The game includes the software development kits (SDKs) below. They send data from your device to Google, encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS). Test builds handed to testers before these SDKs were added do not contain them; this policy covers the full set the game uses.

3.1 Google AdMob (rewarded ads)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward (reroll a card, refill energy, get a tool, revive). The AdMob SDK starts when the game opens so an ad can be ready. Google states that the SDK collects:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses and shares this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We ask AdMob to show only ads suitable for teens and adults.

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, AdMob data disclosure.

3.2 Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played and where players get stuck, so we can improve it. It collects an app-instance ID, the advertising ID, coarse location derived from a masked IP address, app sessions and screen views, and game events we send, for example that a run started or ended (with island and wave number) or that an ad was offered, shown, rewarded or failed.

3.3 Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

When the game crashes, Crashlytics collects the stack trace, the relevant app state, device information at the time of the crash (such as model and Android version) and a Crashlytics installation ID. We use it only to find and fix crashes.

More: Privacy and security in Firebase.

Google's own privacy policy applies to all three services: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested. Your choice decides whether ads may be personalised. Where consent is required, Firebase Analytics stays off unless you consent. Crash reports are still collected so we can fix crashes that affect everyone. If the game shows a "Privacy options" entry in Settings, you can use it to change your choice at any time; you can also email us.

5. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). After deleting it, apps receive no advertising ID.
  • Ad personalisation: manage it in My Ad Center.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game.

6. Sharing

We have no servers and do not receive your personal data directly. Apart from the Google services in section 3, we do not share data with anyone. We see only aggregated statistics and crash reports in the Firebase console.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Save file: stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash data and its identifiers are kept for 90 days, then removed.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level data is kept for at most 14 months (the retention setting in our project is 2 or 14 months).
  • Google AdMob: kept according to Google's retention policy.

Because the game has no accounts, we cannot link analytics or ad data to a person, so we cannot look it up or delete it for you. To remove what is on your device, uninstall the game or clear its storage. To break the link to past ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID as shown above. Questions about your data: email us.

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child under 16 has used the game, uninstall it from their device and reset the advertising ID as shown above; you can email us with any question.

9. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location. It uses vibration for haptic feedback (Settings > Vibration turns it off). It declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) so AdMob can read the advertising ID, and internet access for the services above.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
